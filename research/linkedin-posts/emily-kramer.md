Post Text: MCPs will increasingly be used as distribution for content and data…not just product functionality.

Yesterday Ramp launched the Ramp Data MCP.

A few hours earlier, I hit send on a newsletter talking about how we should be distributing content this way.

Serendipity? Sign of a trend? All of the above?

What the Ramp Data MCP does: 
Gives you aggregated, anonymized spend data from 50,000+ companies, available as a Claude connector, API, and MCP. You can ask Claude what businesses are actually spending on, which categories are growing, which vendors people are switching from. 
Notably, this is separate from the Ramp MCP that gives access to product functionality.

What the MKT1 MCP does: 
Gives you access to our newsletter content, skills I built based on our frameworks and content, and our State of Marketing research data.

Why this matters: 
Most MCP launches so far have been about connecting products to Claude Code. Some of my most-used MCPs are Clay, Figma, Airtable, Attio, and Mutiny.

But the same plumbing works for distributing content and research.

There's a huge opportunity for companies who target AI-pilled (is that the phrase I'm to use here!?) audiences to turn their data sets, content libraries, and frameworks over to their audience in Claude Code.

Content becomes so much more useful when companies (or newsletter creators!) share it in Claude where you can build and expand on it yourself.

You can even imagine a full free tier that's just a Claude MCP or plugin, kind of like what Ramp is doing with their data.

⛽ In MKT1 terms: Claude is where you create fuel, but increasingly it's a new engine for distributing fuel too.
