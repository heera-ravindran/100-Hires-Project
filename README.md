Chosen Topic: B2B SaaS Email Marketing & Newsletter Research

## Why This Topic:
Email is owned media — the list belongs to you, not an algorithm
Sits at the intersection of demand gen, content, and retention
One of the most measurable and compounding channels in B2B SaaS
Undergoing real structural change — AI inbox triage, unreliable open rates, shift from brand to personal newsletters

## 10 Experts Selected

## What Has Been Collected

## LinkedIn Posts
Collected via Apify LinkedIn Profile Posts Scraper
Raw dataset: 200 posts across 10 expert profiles
After filtering for substantive email/newsletter content: 42 posts retained
Processed using Python in Cursor IDE with Claude Code
Saved as structured Markdown files per author in /research/linkedin-posts/

## YouTube Transcripts
Collected transcripts from videos published by experts across the list
Focused on videos where the expert goes deep on email strategy, newsletter operations, or specific tactics
Transcripts saved per video in /research/youtube-transcripts/ with source URL and annotation
This source type provides significantly more depth than LinkedIn posts — full reasoning, data points, and step-by-step execution detail

## Other Sources
Newsletter issues collected from public archives including MKT1, Growth Unhinged, and Total Annarchy
Podcast episode notes from relevant Exit Five sessions on email and newsletter marketing
Additional articles and conference session material where available
All saved in /research/other/ organised by expert and source type
